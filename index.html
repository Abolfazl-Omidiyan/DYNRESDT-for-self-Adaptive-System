DYNRESDT Self-Adaptation & Penalty-Guided Capacity Scaling

Hospital wards handle emergency surges by adapting their physical space. If load exceeds constraints, the MAPE-K Feedback loop evaluates candidate adaptation strategies like temporary zones. A penalty-guided utility function balances operational costs against safety penalties ($300/hr for queue, $80-$160/hr for non-standard beds) to select the optimal state and avoid thrashing.
